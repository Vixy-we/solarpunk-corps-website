Day 2 · 3 to 6 PM

AI, computer vision & simulation

Day 2 turned the foundation into software. Machine learning basics led into OpenCV and MediaPipe, camera-based systems and circuit simulation in Tinkercad and Wokwi. Teams then built small working projects around a real-world or sustainability challenge.

04

Day 4 · 10 AM to 6 PM

Hardware buildathon

The last day was a full build. Each team received a problem statement and 100 credits for the Component Marketplace, then designed, built and programmed a prototype in the morning. The afternoon went to integration, testing, debugging and the final pitch.

The day, in order

  1. 10 AM to 1 PM · Part I

    Problem statement and 100 credits. Visit the Component Marketplace, then design, build and program the prototype.

  2. 3 to 6 PM · Part II

    Integrate, test and debug. Demo the working prototype.

  3. Showcase

    Pitch the problem, the solution, the resources used, the impact, the SDG connection and future scope.

  4. Close

    Judging, results and closure.

How to add photos and videos

  • Each frame lists what to shoot. The list below tracks what is filled.

Frames

More ideas to add

  • Room tone: a 20 second ambient audio clip per day with a play button.
  • Build log: one card per team with three photos and a single sentence.
  • Quote notes: short lines from participants as sticky notes on the wall.
  • Hall map: a floor plan with pins that open the photo taken at that spot.
  • Sketch board: scans of whiteboard and notebook plans in a corkboard strip.
  • Counter strip: participants, teams and prototypes, once the numbers are confirmed.
  • Thank-you strip: faculty, volunteers and the executive team with a face and a role.
